Milepost

A mileage log built around the one field a tracker cannot fill in.

Automatic trackers record the drive. They cannot record why you went — and that is the field most often missing when a mileage claim is questioned. Milepost asks for it first, tells you when the answer is too vague to identify the trip, and keeps the whole year in a form you can hand to an accountant.

No account, no server, and no location permission at all. Nothing follows you around, and no drive is lost because you started it out of coverage.

Milepost is not your accountant

Milepost multiplies the distance you enter by the rate you set, and lays the result out on a page. It ships with no rates, because they change every year and differ by country. It does not give tax advice, does not file anything, and does not know which categories you may claim or what records you are required to keep. Check that with your tax authority or your accountant.
