Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026

The short version. Milepost does not collect your data. There is no account, no server, no analytics — and, unusually for a mileage app, no location permission. Milepost never asks where you are and has no way to find out. Your trips, places and totals are stored on your phone and never leave it, except in the files you deliberately export.

1. Who this policy is from

Milepost is developed by Nguyen Anh Tuan ("we"). Contact: 1551010058.linh+milepost@gmail.com

2. What Milepost stores, and where

Milepost stores the trips you enter — date, purpose, start and destination, distance, category, any odometer readings and notes — along with your vehicles, your rates and your settings. All of it is written to your device's local storage. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. We have no database, no server, and no way to read it.

A mileage log is a record of your movements, which is sensitive whether or not the law calls it that. That is the reason it stays on the device.

3. What we do not collect

  • No location access. Milepost does not request or use Location Services, background location, or significant-change monitoring.
  • No account, email address, or profile.
  • No analytics, usage tracking, or event logging.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No contacts, photos, calendar, or motion data.

4. Files you export

When you export a PDF or CSV, Milepost writes it to a temporary folder on your device and hands it to whatever you choose next — Mail, Files, a printer, a messaging app. Where it goes after that is governed by the app you sent it with, not by Milepost. An exported log is plain, readable data about your movements; treat it accordingly.

5. Notifications

The optional evening reminder is scheduled entirely on your device by iOS. Nothing is sent through a push server, and no reminder ever leaves the phone.

6. Payments

The Milepost subscription is sold by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never see your payment details, and receive only aggregated, non-identifying sales reports.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your records stay on your device until you delete them. Deleting the app removes them.

Because there is no server, we cannot restore anything. If the phone is lost, broken or reset, the only copies are your device backup and the files you have already exported. Tax authorities commonly expect mileage records to be kept for several years — do not let a single phone be the only place they exist. Export at least once a quarter.

8. Children

Milepost is a record-keeping tool for people who drive for work and is not directed to children.

9. Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights over personal data held about you. We hold none. Your records live on your device under your control. We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not have any.

10. Changes to this policy

The current version is always at https://inat97.github.io/milepost/privacy.html.
